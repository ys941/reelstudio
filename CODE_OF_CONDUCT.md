# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind. Assume good faith. Remember that the person on the other side is
probably tired, probably learning, and probably doing this in their spare time.

That covers about 99% of it. The rest is detail.

## What's expected

- **Be patient with beginners.** Everyone was one. This project was itself built
  by someone learning as they went, and it shows in places.
- **Critique the code, not the person.** "This breaks when the input is empty"
  is useful. "This is terrible" is not.
- **Accept that people have less time than you'd like.** Reviews may take days.
  A nudge after a week is fine; five nudges in two days is not.
- **Say when you don't know.** "I'm not sure this is right, but…" is a strength.

## What's not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any sort.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or pull requests.
- Discrimination based on age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
  expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or
  sexual identity and orientation.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any
other space where this project is being represented.

## Enforcement

Report anything that needs attention to **ys9410017064@gmail.com**. Reports are handled
privately. Depending on severity, the response ranges from a request to stop,
through removal of the offending content, to a permanent ban from the project.

The maintainer has the final say, and will try to be fair about it.
